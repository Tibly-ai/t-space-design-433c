THE MANIFESTO

We reject superficial ornament. True luxury lives in raw materiality, precise junctions, and shadow.

Every site has a natural volume and a local light profile. We do not impose forms, but rather uncover the architecture that should already belong there. Grounded in the Pacific Northwest, our work spans deep concrete foundations, local cedar timbers, and expansive glass panels.
