Selected
Works

An ongoing inquiry into volume, materiality, and pure geometry. Each commission represents a rigorous distillation of shelter, designed to capture the changing qualities of natural light over raw, unadorned surfaces.

01 / RESIDENCE

The Monolith House

Constructed of site-cast board-marked concrete on a remote coastal headland. The design relies on single aperture windows to draw focused beams of afternoon sun across structural concrete slabs.

02 / SPAATIAL

Symmetry Gallery

A private exhibition space defined by a single central structural column. Polished screed floors and raw lime plaster walls create an atmosphere of monastic calm.

03 / RETREAT

Forest Pavilion

A seasonal sanctuary balancing a heavy dark steel superstructure with high-transparency low-iron glass panels. Designed to visually dissolve into the damp Oregon wilderness.
