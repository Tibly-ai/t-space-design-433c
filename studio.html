01 / Foundation

Quiet volumes,
uncompromised
purity.

T-Space Design Studio was founded on the conviction that architectural excellence is achieved by stripping away the non-essential. We treat light as a physical material, space as a sculptural canvas, and site conditions as an unyielding template for form.

ESTABLISHED IN PORTLAND, OREGON

02 / Methodology

How We Frame Space

02.1 / Structural Integrity

Honesty in Raw Materials

We reject applied ornamentation. Concrete, structural steel, rough cedar, and monolithic glass are detailed to expose their true load-bearing qualities. Every joint, seam, and alignment serves both structural truth and minimal composition.

02.2 / Micro-Climate Analysis

Responsive Light Capture

Before drawing a single wall, we study the seasonal passage of daylight across the terrain. Windows are positioned not for facade symmetry, but to project evolving paintings of light and long shadows onto structural interior surfaces throughout the day.

02.3 / Tectonic Restraint

The Power of the Void

A room is defined by what is absent. We design built-in concealment systems for mechanical elements, lighting fixtures, and storage, leaving architectural volumes completely clean and free from visual distraction.
